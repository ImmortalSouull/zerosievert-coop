[size=4][b]What is it[/b][/size]
A co-op mod for ZERO Sievert. You and your friends go into the same raid: one map, the same enemies, the same loot, you see and hear each other. Instead of dying you go down, and a teammate can pick you up. Invite friends through Steam or connect by IP. Every player needs the mod (same version).

[size=4][b]New in 2.0.0[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Up to 8 players[/b] in one session (was 4).
[*][b]Voice chat[/b] (hold C or open mic): quieter with distance, silent far away.
[*][b]Radio[/b] - a new item sold by the barman (Loners reputation 400) or crafted in the workshop. With the radio in hand you talk on its channel at any distance.
[*][b]Arena in the bunker[/b]: PvP in the shooting range with your own gear or a preset weapon. Nothing is lost, a scoreboard at the end.
[*][b]Trading[/b] between players (U), and a look at a teammate's gear (I).
[*][b]The session survives the host leaving[/b]: another player takes over, the raid goes on. Rejoin after a crash.
[*][b]Crawling and dragging[/b]: a downed player crawls; a teammate can drag them to cover and revive them there.
[*][b]Group extraction[/b] with a countdown, and a [b]raid summary[/b] (kills, damage, value, revives).
[*][b]Spectating[/b] your teammates after the final death.
[*][b]Text chat[/b] (Enter) and a [b]command wheel[/b] (hold Z): "Follow me", "Enemy spotted", "Need ammo"...
[*][b]Group panel[/b]: teammates' HP, distance, weapon and current action.
[*][b]Raid events[/b]: a call for help on the map - hold out against waves, clear an area or take down a commander.
[*][b]Shared quest progress[/b] (rewards split by the work) and [b]group tasks at the barman[/b].
[*][b]Personal loot[/b] option, [b]friendly fire[/b] setting, difficulty that grows with the group.
[*]A fallen teammate's things go back to them after you extract.
[*][b]Lobby management[/b] (kick, friends only / invite only / public) and a list of open games.
[*][b]Save backups[/b] before every co-op session, one-click updates, a "Collect logs" button, streamer mode.
[*]Every new text is translated into all languages of the game.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Features since 1.x[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Shared raids: the same map, enemies, containers, bodies and loot for everybody.
[*]Downs and revives: lethal damage puts you down; a teammate revives you with nothing, a bandage or a medkit.
[*]Join a raid that is already running (the train), hub lobby and visits to a friend's bunker.
[*]Ping marks, teammates on the PDA map, name tags.
[*]Co-op settings in the game's difficulty menu.
[*]High refresh rate monitors supported.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Close the game. ZERO Sievert [b]1.4.13[/b] (Steam) is required.
[*]Download the file, unpack it anywhere and run [b]install.bat[/b]. The installer finds the game, makes a backup and patches your copy of the game. It installs over an older version of the mod.
[*]In the game: main menu - "Co-op" (or [b]F7[/b]). Host via Steam and invite your friends, or connect by IP (UDP port 47777).
[*]Uninstall: [b]uninstall.bat[/b], or Steam - Properties - "Verify integrity of game files".
[/list]
Your saves are backed up before every co-op session (F7 - Save backups).

[size=4][b]Known issues[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Over Steam, more than 2 players at once, host migration and voice chat were checked by automated tests only. If something goes wrong, use "Collect logs" in F7 and send the folder.
[*]The Epic Games Store version is not supported.
[*]An enemy outside a guest's screen may open fire on the guest right away (this rule lives in a part of the game's code the mod can't change).
[*]The radio exists only with the mod: without it the game removes the radio from your inventory (the save stays fine).
[/list]
